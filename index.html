Players alternate picking a piece for the other player to place on the 4×4 board. Align four sharing any attribute to declare Quarto.
Quick rules
  • Pieces have four binary attributes: tall/short, light/dark, round/square, solid/hollow.
  • On your turn, pick one unused piece to hand to your opponent.
  • Your opponent must place that piece on any empty square.
  • Four in a line sharing any attribute wins instantly. If all pieces are placed with no line, it's a draw.
